Summary

What worked and what did not

Worked

  • ✓Rules engine for the supplied rules plus a 13-point convention set (ADR 0001), with 29 passing tests covering conservation, calls, special wins and hidden information.
  • ✓Playing agent: a masked recurrent PPO policy passes Gate 1 with no illegal actions.
  • ✓Perception: per-tile positions in a close-packed row to about 0.2 mm; tile recognition 98.1%, with misreads flagged by low confidence.
  • ✓Five tile operations in physics, including the staged side-regrasp tip-over that lands tiles within about 2 mm of their slot.
  • ✓End-to-end loop: perception, belief, policy, binding, execution, verification and reconciliation complete 72% of held-out simulated hands.

Did not work, or not done

  • ✕Hardware gates 2 and 4 are not run; this environment has no robot.
  • ✕The hybrid misses Gate 3 (66/100). Its learned contact policy did not improve on open-loop contact in this simulator.
  • ✕Learned-only fails every hand: without a planner its approach policy drives the arm into joint limits, even after DAgger.
  • ✕Across-table identity: opponents' tiles read below 0.8 confidence cause 15 to 18 of the failed hands.
  • ✕Strategy strength: the policy wins 13.4% against 30.3% for the fixed heuristic in the same seat.
Remaining work

What needs to be done

Grouped by what unblocks what. Hardware bring-up comes first because both physical gates depend on it and several simulation assumptions need checking against it.

1. Bring up the hardware and check the simulation's assumptions

Blocks gates 2 and 4
  1. Gripper direction. Command joint7 = 0 and see whether the fingers close. The upstream meshes say closed; Agentic's contact model assumes open.
  2. Pad and tile sizes. Measure the fingertip width (about 15 mm assumed) and your tiles (20 × 26 × 16 mm, 12 g assumed), then update sim/layout.py.
  3. Camera calibration. Calibrate the D435i extrinsic and the D405 hand-eye transform. Simulation assumes about 1 mm / 0.1° and 0.5 mm / 0.3° error.
  4. Mount and mark the table as in sim/layout.py: hand anchor, pond and meld slots, staging spot and shared zone.
  5. Build the hardware driver layer (i2rt YAM control, RealSense capture) behind the same interfaces the simulator uses. It does not exist yet.

2. Run Gate 2 on the robot

Gate 2
  1. Collect operator-guided demonstrations for the contact skills and fine-tune the contact policy on them.
  2. Re-run the hybrid selection (ADR 0005) on hardware development trials; real contact may favour the learned policy where the simulator did not.
  3. Run 50 held-out trials per skill and 10 views per tile type, logged with mahjong-robot trial add --gate 2 (docs/gate2-protocol.md).

3. Raise Gate 3 completion and make the hybrid pass

Gate 3
  1. Across-table identity (largest cause): add a second overhead view or a higher-resolution crop for the far ponds, and use a wrist-camera look before faulting.
  2. Claimed tile not found in the shared zone (4 hands): diagnose from the fault dumps in reports/gate3/final/faults/.
  3. Tip-over landing errors (5 to 6 hands): add a corrective push when a tile lands off its slot instead of faulting.
  4. Contact policy: DAgger in physics on the states the policy visits, and a wrist-camera mount that does not lose the row end behind the held tile.
  5. Learned-only: add joint-limit awareness or a joint-space action space to the approach policy so the ablation is a real comparison.
  6. Re-run all three modes on a fresh held-out block after any change, as with block 1.

4. Run Gate 4 physical play

Gate 4
  1. Five complete hands with humans dealing and presenting tiles per docs/gate4-protocol.md; no corrective touch.
  2. Ten trials each of Chow, Pung, exposed, concealed and added Kong, self-draw win and win by discard.

5. Optional: a stronger playing agent

Not required
  1. Close the gap to the heuristic (13.4% against 30.3%) with longer training, imitation warm-start or search. Gate 1 only requires beating random play.
